## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered for aesthetic clinics, laser hair removal centers, and premium beauty spas. It balances the warmth, serenity, and refinement of high-end wellness hospitality with the high-throughput operational precision of a mission-critical SaaS CRM.

The design movement combines **Contemporary Clean Minimalism** with **Clinical Softness**:
- **Tone**: Polished, tranquil, organized, and reliable.
- **Aesthetic Attributes**: Subdued sage-mint highlights, deep petroleum teal structures, luminous warm-gray backgrounds, and surgical precision in interface density.
- **Atmosphere**: Eliminates visual clutter to alleviate receptionist and clinician cognitive fatigue while preserving an upscale, spa-like experience during client-facing interactions.

## Colors

The palette establishes an authoritative, soothing visual hierarchy:

- **Primary (`#0D5C58`) & Tertiary (`#147B74`)**: Deep petroleum teal provides clinical authority, high contrast against white surfaces, and an elevated cosmetic identity. Used for primary calls-to-action, navigation anchors, active state highlights, and structural emphasis.
- **Secondary (`#2DD4BF`) & Mint Support (`#E6F4F1`)**: Soft sage and radiant mint evoke hygiene, rejuvenation, and comfort. Utilized for secondary badges, subtle active fills, and gentle focus indicators.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F8FAFC`, `#F1F5F9`, `#FFFFFF`)**: Warm, breathable off-whites prevent clinical sterility while giving calendar grids and data tables an uncluttered foundation.
- **Typography Neutrals (`#1E293B`, `#334155`, `#64748B`)**: Graphite and slate tones replace pure black to maintain soft, continuous legibility throughout 8-hour operational shifts.
- **Operational Status Palette**: Explicit semantic tokens communicate agenda statuses instantly without cognitive strain:
  - `status-success` (`#10B981`): Confirmed / Completed / Paid
  - `status-pending` (`#F59E0B`): Pending / Awaiting Confirmation
  - `status-canceled` (`#EF4444`): Canceled / No-show
  - `status-info` (`#3B82F6`): Rescheduled / Inquiry
  - `status-in-progress` (`#8B5CF6`): In Session / Checked-In

## Typography

Inter serves as the unified typographic engine across all levels. Its tall x-height, neutral geometric apertures, and extensive tabular numeral support ensure effortless scanning of operational schedules, procedural timetables, invoice calculations, and client dossiers.

- **Headlines & Metric Figures**: Weighted in Semibold (`600`) and Bold (`700`) with subtle negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) to convey modern authority and crisp presence.
- **Body Text**: Tuned for maximum legibility at `14px` (`body-md`) and `16px` (`body-lg`) using `1.4` to `1.5` line-height multiples.
- **Micro-Copy & Badges**: Labels and status chips leverage uppercase or medium-weight tracking (`0.02em` to `0.04em`) to ensure instant categorization in dense schedule matrix views.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is governed by an **8px base grid** with a 12-column fluid structure for desktop admin dashboards and responsive column collapse for tablets and mobile devices.

- **Breakpoints**:
  - `Desktop (Wide)`: >= 1440px (Sidebar fixed at 260px, fluid content area, 24px gutters).
  - `Laptop / Compact Desktop`: 1024px – 1439px (Sidebar collapsible to 72px icon rail, 20px gutters).
  - `Tablet`: 768px – 1023px (Sidebar drawer, 2-column card layouts, 16px gutters).
  - `Mobile`: < 768px (Single column stacked, bottom navigation bar, 16px outer margin).
- **Rhythm Rules**:
  - Compact operational components (appointment cells, table rows, form inputs) use `space-sm` (8px) and `space-md` (16px) internal padding.
  - Informational hierarchy between cards and sections relies on `space-lg` (24px) and `space-xl` (32px) margins to preserve a tranquil, uncrowded feel.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered tonal surfaces complemented by soft, diffuse ambient shadows tinted slightly with slate and teal undertones:

- **Level 0 (Base)**: `#F8FAFC` canvas. Flat, anchor layer.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Surfaces)**: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with a delicate 1px border (`#E2E8F0`) and subtle ambient shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02);`
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Popovers & Hover States)**: Elevated utility controls use an expanded diffusion:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04);`
- **Level 3 (Modals, Slide-overs & Flyouts)**: High priority overlays with focused depth:
  `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(13, 92, 88, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04);`
- **Interactive Depth Rule**: Hover states on interactive cards should not shift spatial position violently; they transition border tone from `#E2E8F0` to `#2DD4BF` and raise shadow to Level 2 over 150ms.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a unified **12px to 14px corner radius** hierarchy (`roundedness: 2`), reflecting the organic softness of aesthetics while staying disciplined and structural:

- **Cards, Panels & Modals**: `14px` (`border-radius: 0.875rem`). Softens high-density interfaces without sacrificing grid efficiency.
- **Buttons, Text Inputs & Dropdown Triggers**: `10px` to `12px` (`border-radius: 0.625rem` to `0.75rem`). Ensures ergonomic finger-tap targets and visual balance.
- **Status Pills, Tags & Avatar Chips**: Fully rounded pill shapes (`border-radius: 9999px`) to contrast cleanly with rectangular content cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Background `#0D5C58`, text `#FFFFFF`, border none, border-radius `10px`. Hover state shifts to `#147B74` with slight scale (`1.01`). Active state deepens to `#0A4845`.
- **Secondary / Subtle**: Background `#E6F4F1`, text `#0D5C58`, border none. Hover state changes background to `#D1ECE6`.
- **Outline / Ghost**: Background transparent, border 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, text `#1E293B`. Hover state applies background `#F1F5F9` and border `#CBD5E1`.
- **Height Dimensions**: Small (32px), Default (40px), Large (48px for POS and touch check-in).

### Status Chips & Badges
- Built as pill shapes with `label-sm` typography (11px, weight 600).
- Configured using a subtle 10-15% opacity background with a saturated text tint:
  - *Confirmed*: Background `#ECFDF5`, Text `#065F46`
  - *In Service*: Background `#F5F3FF`, Text `#5B21B6`
  - *Pending*: Background `#FFFBEB`, Text `#92400E`
  - *Canceled*: Background `#FEF2F2`, Text `#991B1B`

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Form Inputs**: Height 40px, background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, corner radius `10px`, text `#1E293B`. Placeholder in `#94A3B8`.
- **Focus State**: Border `#0D5C58` accompanied by an outer ring glow of `0 0 0 3px rgba(45, 212, 191, 0.25)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 18px box size with 4px corner radius for checkboxes and full radius for radios. Checked fill `#0D5C58` with crisp white checkmark icon.

### Cards & Container Panels
- White `#FFFFFF` background, 1px solid `#E2E8F0` border, `14px` border-radius.
- Internal padding adheres strictly to `space-md` (16px) for data tables and `space-lg` (24px) for summaries and settings blocks.

### Specialized CRM Domain Components
- **Appointment Slot Card (Calendar Matrix)**:
  - Left border colored with procedure/status semantic bar (4px thick).
  - Background `#FFFFFF` with `#F8FAFC` secondary metadata row.
  - Quick-action buttons (WhatsApp alert, status change, invoice) appear on hover in an elevated micro-action toolbar.
- **Client Dossier Header**:
  - Horizontal bar featuring client portrait, medical history tags (allergies, skin phototype), lifetime value (LTV), and upcoming package session count.
